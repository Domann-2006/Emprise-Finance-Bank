## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of a resilient, modern financial institution combining century-old civic dependability with digital precision. The target audience encompasses commercial business owners, personal retail depositors, and digital-first wealth management clients seeking uncompromising clarity, security, and effortless transacting.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** integrity with **Precision Minimalism**:
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts utilize generous negative space, crisp modular containers, and structural content boundaries to eliminate financial anxiety and cognitive fatigue.
- **Civic Trust:** Rooted in authoritative deep maritime teal-navy anchors, energized by luminous glacial cyan accents and energetic coral touchpoints.
- **Calculated Dignity:** Avoids whimsical or distracting micro-animations. Motion is deliberate, swift, and serves strict transactional confirmation and state clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of institutional solvency, transparency, and ergonomic focus:
- **Primary (`#002D3F`):** Deep oceanic navy anchoring navigation bars, primary calls-to-action, high-emphasis text, and core structural surfaces. Conveys authority and legacy reliability.
- **Secondary (`#9EE6E8`):** Glacial cyan used for high-visibility highlights, active navigation indicators, subtle backdrop illumination, and secondary feature flags.
- **Tertiary (`#FC4F38`):** Vivid warm coral serving as the decisive operational alert and critical conversion driver (e.g., immediate transfer confirmation, fraud flags, or high-urgency notifications).
- **Neutral (`#5A6E77`):** Cool slate gray balancing typography, borders, metadata, and lower-emphasis secondary information without harsh contrast fatigue.
- **Surfaces (`#F4F7F9` and `#FFFFFF`):** Cool, crisp backgrounds paired with pure white elevated modules providing immaculate financial data demarcation.

## Typography

Typography relies on **IBM Plex Sans** across all hierarchies to reflect industrial-grade precision, exceptional tabular numeral legibility, and technical confidence.

Key execution rules:
- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply OpenType tabular figure variants (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`) to all monetary balances, transaction ledgers, account numbers, and percentage yields.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Large headlines and display numbers must employ confident weights (`600` or `700`) paired with tight line heights to maintain visual cohesion across dashboards.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Field identifiers, micro-disclosures, and category tags utilize uppercase tracking (`+0.5px` to `+1px`) in medium-to-semibold weights for rapid visual scanning across dense ledgers.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural framework is built on an adaptable 8-point harmonic rhythm deployed via an asymmetrical 12-column responsive fluid grid:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, 24px gutters, max canvas width 1360px centered with 40px outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 24px outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 16px margins with edge-to-edge scrolling balance cards.

Layout logic mandates strict container-based padding: standard cards use `space-lg` (24px) for desktop and `space-md` (16px) for mobile. Dense tabular statements scale down to `space-sm` (8px) vertical row spacing to maximize visible line items without claustrophobic crowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **Tonal Layering & Soft Ambient Shadow** model. Rather than harsh black drop-shadows, depth is expressed through subtle marine-tinted ambient diffusions:

- **Surface Floor (Canvas):** Grounded at `#F4F7F9` to create a cool canvas against which white cards achieve immediate optical contrast.
- **Elevation Level 1 (Static Cards / Tables):** White surface (`#FFFFFF`), surrounded by a 1px structural border of `#DCE5E8` and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 45, 63, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 45, 63, 0.03)`.
- **Elevation Level 2 (Hovered Cards / Interactive Widgets):** Smooth transition upward: `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 45, 63, 0.07)`, 1px border colored `#CBD8DC`.
- **Elevation Level 3 (Drawers / Popovers / Modals):** Crisp white surface backed by a high-diffusion elevation: `0 16px 40px rgba(0, 29, 41, 0.14)`. Backdrop dimming uses `#001D29` at 55% opacity with 4px backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a balanced `roundedness: 2` (8px base radius) standard that combines professional discipline with modern approachability:
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Dropdowns:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px) border radius to maintain tactile predictability.
- **Cards, Modals, and Elevated Panels:** Scaled up to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to visually contain multi-component financial clusters.
- **Interactive Badges and Pill Counters:** Set to fully rounded (9999px) strictly for statuses, quick tags, and live notification dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#002D3F`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px border radius, font weight 600. On hover, background shifts to `#05435B`. Active state applies a scale transform of 0.98. Focus rings use `#9EE6E8` with a 3px outer spread.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#002D3F`, text `#002D3F`. Hover initiates a background fill of `#EAF4F6`.
- **Accent / Alert:** Background `#FC4F38`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved strictly for primary irreversible commitments, account locking, or urgent monetary transfers.

### Cards & Account Tiles
- Pure white container with a 1px border (`#DCE5E8`), 12px border radius, and Level 1 elevation.
- Header bands on premium accounts feature a subtle top accent bar (3px) in `#9EE6E8` or `#002D3F`. Account balance values render in IBM Plex Sans Bold with explicit currency spacing.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Controls:** 44px minimum touch height, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#DCE5E8`, 8px corner radius. Focused inputs shift border to `#002D3F` with an accompanying 2px glow ring in `#9EE6E8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px controls with a 2px `#5A6E77` border. When checked, fill with `#002D3F` carrying a crisp white glyph check or radio dot.

### Chips & Status Indicators
- **Positive Status (Cleared, Active):** Background `#E3FCEF`, text `#00875A`, 4px border radius.
- **Pending / Warning:** Background `#FFF0B3`, text `#E8890C`.
- **Critical / Action Required:** Background `#FFEBE6`, text `#FC4F38`.

### Transaction Lists
- Zebra striping is deprecated in favor of clean 1px bottom separators (`#DCE5E8`).
- Hover states on list rows apply a soft `#F4F7F9` transition with full-row clickability. Positive deposits display bold `#00875A` figures; standard debits default to `#002D3F`.
